# Sources and Modelling Conventions

## Public sources

1. [Squarespace FY2023 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1496963/000149696324000007/sqsp-02282024x8kexhibit991.htm) - FY2023 and FY2022 revenue, adjusted EBITDA, cash flow, debt, cash, and operating KPIs.
2. [Squarespace FY2022 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1496963/000149696323000010/sqsp-20221231.htm) - FY2021 historical financial data.
3. [Permira tender-offer presentation](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1496963/000114036124040628/ny20030653x14_ex99-3.htm) - revised $46.50 per-share offer and approximately $7.2bn enterprise value.
4. [Permira transaction-completion release](https://www.squarespace.com/press-releases/2024/10/17/permira-completes-acquisition-of-squarespace) - 17 October 2024 completion date.

## Conventions

- Financial values are in US dollars and presented in millions unless otherwise noted.
- FY2021 to FY2023 are historical, public figures.
- FY2024E to FY2028E are case-study forecasts.
- Transaction value, offer price, and historical financials are public-source inputs.
- New debt, interest rate, shareholder rollover, transaction fees, cash tax rate, working-capital investment, operating cases, and exit multiple are illustrative assumptions.
- The model uses a full cash sweep and does not recreate undisclosed transaction financing or ownership arrangements.
